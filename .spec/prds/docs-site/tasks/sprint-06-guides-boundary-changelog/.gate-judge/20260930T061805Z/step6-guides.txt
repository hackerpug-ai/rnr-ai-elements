Guides

Task-oriented how-to guides for the installed items: re-theme your app, update installed items, preview on a device, customize rendering, and troubleshoot failed installs.

All guides
5
Re-theme your app

Change the tokens your installed items read — fonts, radius, and colors — in the engine tree your project uses, without touching the copied item files.

Update installed items

Re-run the absolute-URL add command to bring installed items back in sync with the registry, and answer the CLI overwrite prompt on purpose.

Preview on a device

Run your app with installed items in Expo Go on a physical device, and know which opt-in items need a dev client before they will load.

Customize rendering

Take over how message and reasoning bodies render through the renderMarkdown prop, and restyle nested text through the TextClassContext provider.

Troubleshooting

Four symptom-cause-fix entries for failed installs: web components from short names, missing styles from the wrong engine tree, Expo Go crashes on dev-client items, and the renamed Streamdown streaming option.
